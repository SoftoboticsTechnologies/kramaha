Rooted in tradition

Traditional Services

Rooted in time-tested knowledge systems, our traditional services provide deeper insights into life patterns, environments, and energies.

Astrology

Astrology at Kramaha is used as a guidance tool to help individuals understand life cycles, strengths, challenges, and opportunities. It supports informed decision-making related to career, relationships, and personal growth.

Vastu

Our Vastu guidance focuses on creating harmony between individuals and their living or working spaces. By aligning environments with natural energies, we help enhance peace, productivity, and overall well-being.

The whole person

Holistic Services

Our holistic services focus on understanding the individual as a whole—mind, behavior, personality, and expression.

01

Personality Analysis

This service helps uncover behavioral patterns, strengths, emotional responses, and decision-making styles. It enables individuals to gain deeper self-awareness and improve personal and professional interactions.

02

Handwriting Analysis

Handwriting reflects the subconscious mind. Through handwriting analysis, we identify personality traits, emotional tendencies, confidence levels, and areas for personal development.

03

Mannerisms Re-Engineering

This service focuses on identifying unconscious habits, body language, and behavioral patterns. Through guided re-engineering, individuals learn to align their mannerisms with confidence, clarity, and positive self-expression.

04

Business Consultation

Our business consultation service helps individuals and professionals improve decision-making, leadership skills, workplace communication, and overall business growth. We provide practical guidance to enhance confidence, strategic thinking, and professional success.

05

Graphology for Holistic Development

Graphology helps understand personality traits, emotional patterns, strengths, and behavioral tendencies through handwriting analysis. This service supports personal growth, self-awareness, confidence building, and holistic development for both personal and professional life.
