About

My Story

With over a decade of experience in learning & design and development of assessment centers leadership training, and organizational excellence, B. V. Kaushik brings deep insight into human behavior, performance, and growth.

Kramaha was created to take this expertise beyond organizations and make it accessible to individuals seeking clarity, confidence, and direction in life and work.

Kramaha is built on the belief that clarity leads to action, and action leads to transformation.

We focus on developing people by strengthening their thinking patterns, emotional resilience, decision-making, and professional capabilities. Our interventions are designed to be simple, scalable, and sustainable.
